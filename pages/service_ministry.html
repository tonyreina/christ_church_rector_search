Service & Outreach

Bridging love and service

Our Service Ministry helps our parish live an active, visible faith. Meeting monthly, this core group of volunteers plans outreach efforts for the church community. Our parish community not only provides financial support as it offers hands-on opportunities, allowing for involvement in our spiritual call to help others. While some activities are held at church, others involve heading over the bridge to St. Mark's and St. Luke's parishes to provide support for the unhoused and those in need.

As James 2:17 reminds us, "faith without works is dead". Guided by that call, we raise funds for Vida Joven through our annual festive Cinco de Mayo dinner, which provides shelter for orphans in Tijuana and Northern Mexico. The Backpack Drive provides over 200 backpacks filled with school supplies for children, and our Thanksgiving Drive works with Christ Church Day School to collect food and financial donations to provide food for 200 families. We share the joy of Advent by providing Christmas gifts for the Episcopal Community Services Head Start programs. And our Lenten Drive annually supports causes the Service Ministry team identifies to serve those in need.

In recent years, we revived "Blessings in a Bag", a tradition of practical care for neighbors experiencing homelessness by equipping parishioners with small gift bags of food and essentials to share with dignity and kindness. Our Thrift Shop, by raising over $100,000/year, extends this outreach as a community ministry, supported by volunteers and donors beyond our congregation. Ongoing, Christ Church partners with St. Mark's to support their Food Pantry, now serving 400 families weekly.

Peace & Justice Committee

Christ Church also has a Peace & Justice Committee. During COVID, parishioners participated in the Episcopal Church's Sacred Ground program, which deepened our commitment to faithful action and honest conversation. From that work, we sensed a call to engage issues affecting our neighbors, and we joined the San Diego Organizing Project (SDOP), a non-partisan, multi-faith network. Through listening and discernment, we focused first on housing, homelessness, and ocean pollution, supporting efforts such as renter protections, housing initiatives, and practical support for the unhoused.

More recently, we have felt a clear call toward immigration and refugee support. We have hosted Home Meetings, participated in forums and rallies, and held discussions on issues including refugee resettlement. In collaboration with Our Lady of Guadalupe Church and SDOP, we now support the FAITH program (Faithful Accompaniment in Trust & Hope), offering moral support and prayerful presence for migrants at court hearings and related sites.
