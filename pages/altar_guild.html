Liturgical Ministry

Altar Guild at Christ Church

The Altar Guild serves as an essential ministry in the life of our parish. Working reverently and quietly behind the scene, members ensure that the altar is prepared and set for every worship service. They also assist in preparing the church for special feast days, baptisms, weddings, funerals, and the services of Holy Week and Easter. Altar Guild duties include caring for the sacred vessels, vestments, and altar linens. Members polish the brass and silver. They clean and maintain the Sanctuary and Sacristy. It is the duty of the Altar Guild to order the bread, wine, oil and supplies that are used for all worship services. In lieu of a Flower Ministry, the Altar Guild coordinates with a local florist to ensure beautiful flower arrangements that have liturgically correct colors for weekly and special services. This ministry is grounded in prayer and attention to detail. The Altar Guild members serve under the direction of the clergy and contribute meaningfully to the reverence, beauty and smooth flow of our common prayer. Through their faithful service, the Altar Guild creates a joyful and sacred space where our parish family gathers to encounter Christ in Word and Sacrament.
